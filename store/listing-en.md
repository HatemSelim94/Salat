# Google Play listing — English

**App name (30):** Salat — Prayer Times

**Short description (80):**
Prayer times and quiet reminders. Offline, no ads, no tracking.

**Full description (4000):**
Salat shows today's prayer times and reminds you at each prayer with a short chime — and otherwise stays out of your way.

LIGHT AND QUIET
• One notification per prayer, with a distinctive short chime you can turn off
• Snooze, and an optional heads-up a few minutes before
• Quiet hours for the night
• About 1 MB, no background service, no battery drain

OFFLINE AND PRIVATE
• Prayer times are calculated on your phone from your city's coordinates
• No internet access, no account, no ads, no analytics — nothing leaves your device
• Choose from 1,800+ cities or enter exact coordinates

EVERYTHING YOU NEED, NOTHING MORE
• Current prayer highlighted, countdown to the next
• Jumu'ah on Fridays with an optional reminder
• Iqamah times
• Ramadan: Suhoor and Iftar countdown
• Qibla compass
• Monthly timetable with CSV export
• Import your mosque's timetable to replace the calculation
• 12 calculation methods (Muslim World League, Egyptian, Umm al-Qura, Karachi, ISNA, …), custom Fajr/Isha angles, Hanafi Asr, high-latitude rules, per-prayer offsets
• Hijri date
• Dark mode, follows your phone's language (English, Arabic, German)

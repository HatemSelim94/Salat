# Salat — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 7 November 2026_

Salat is a prayer-times reminder for Android, Windows, macOS, Linux, Chrome and VS Code, made by Hatem Selim.

## What Salat collects

Nothing. Salat has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. It never connects to the internet.

## What stays on your device

- The city or coordinates you choose, your calculation settings and a mosque timetable you may import. They are stored only in the app's private storage on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the app.
- Prayer times are calculated on the device from those coordinates.

## Permissions

- **Notifications** — to show the prayer reminder.
- **Exact alarms** — so the reminder fires at the prayer time, including while the device sleeps.
- **Receive boot completed** — to re-schedule reminders after a restart.
- **Vibrate** — for the reminder.

Salat does not request location access; you choose the city yourself.

## Children

Salat is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new date.

# Play Console — answers for the questionnaires

**App category:** Lifestyle (or Tools). **Tags:** prayer times, Islam, adhan.

**Privacy policy URL:** host `store/privacy-policy.md` at a public URL (see release notes).

**App access:** All functionality is available without special access (no login).

**Ads:** No, the app does not contain ads.

**Content rating (IARC questionnaire):** Utility/Productivity. Answer "No" to everything (no violence, sexuality, language, controlled substances, gambling, user interaction, sharing of location, purchases). Expect "Everyone / PEGI 3".

**Target audience:** 18 and over (simplest; the app is not designed for children — choosing a child age group triggers the Families policy).

**News app:** No. **COVID-19 app:** No. **Government app:** No. **Financial features:** No. **Health:** No.

**Data safety:**
- Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types? **No.**
- Is all of the user data collected by your app encrypted in transit? — not applicable (nothing collected).
- Do you provide a way for users to request that their data is deleted? — not applicable.
(Chosen city and settings stay in the app's private storage on the device and are never transmitted — that is not "collection" under Play's definition.)

**Permissions declaration (if asked):**
- `USE_EXACT_ALARM` — prayer reminders are time-critical alarms the user explicitly set up (the permission is intended for alarm/calendar apps; Play may ask to confirm this use case).
- `POST_NOTIFICATIONS`, `RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED`, `VIBRATE` — standard.

**Store listing assets:**
- App icon 512×512 PNG (no alpha) — from `tools/make_assets.py` / `android/.../ic_launcher`.
- Feature graphic 1024×500 PNG/JPG — required.
- Phone screenshots: at least 2 (max 8), 16:9 or 9:16, min 320 px — take them on the S21 (home light/dark, settings, month, Qibla).
- 7-inch / 10-inch tablet screenshots — optional for phone-only apps.
